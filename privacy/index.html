MakeEmSmile

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

MakeEmSmile is a party game: a friend tries to make the player smile within one minute while the phone's camera watches the player's face.

What the app uses

  • Camera. Used only while the app is open, to measure facial expressions (smiling, raised eyebrows, yawning and similar) during a round.
  • Player names. Names typed in for the scoreboard stay on the phone.

What happens to your data

  • All face analysis happens on the phone. Camera images and face measurements are never uploaded, sent to us, or shared with anyone by the app.
  • The app does not record video or save photos. A single still frame from the end of a round is kept in memory to show on the results screen and is discarded when the next round starts or the app closes.
  • If you tap Share, the app creates a result picture (including that frame) and hands it to the app you choose, such as a messenger. What happens after that is up to you and that app.
  • The app stores only small game settings on the phone: the best time with its player name, which camera you prefer, and which joke was shown last. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no data is sold or shared.

Comments on this website

  • Comments posted on the comments page are public: your verdict, the name you type (optional) and your comment are shown to everyone, with the time it was posted.
  • They are stored in a Google Sheet run through Google Apps Script, so Google processes them under its own privacy terms. No account, email address or tracking is involved.
  • We may hide or remove comments that are spam, offensive, or share someone's personal information. To have your comment removed, email us.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
