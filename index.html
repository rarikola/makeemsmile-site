MakeEmSmile

You've got 1 minute to make 'em smile.

A party game for Android. One friend keeps a straight face. The other holds the phone and tries everything: faces, jokes, fake farts. The camera knows the exact moment they crack.

▶COMING SOON ONGoogle Play

How it works

  1. Point the phone

    Aim the camera at the player. They can't see the meters, so no cheating.

  2. Make 'em smile

    60 seconds. Pull faces, tell a joke, fart (or fake one). Anything goes.

  3. Crack!

    The moment a smile slips out, a chime plays and the clock stops. Pass the phone and beat their time.
